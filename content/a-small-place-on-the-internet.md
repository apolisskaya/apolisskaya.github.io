This is a notebook, in the loosest sense of the word. A place for ideas to have a little more room than a browser tab, and for projects to leave a trail beyond their source code.

## What belongs here

Writing. Project notes. Questions that turn into experiments. Experiments that turn into better questions.

There’s no particular schedule, and no obligation to turn everything into a lesson. Sometimes a useful observation is enough.

## A work in progress

The categories will grow with the collection. The point is to keep a place for things worth returning to — and, occasionally, to close a few tabs.
